# Product Overview PRD

## Vision
Make security posture and security-relevant changes on a personal Windows PC understandable and actionable.

## Users
- Everyday Windows users who need clear protection status.
- Power users who want event, process and network visibility.
- Privacy-conscious users who want local-only monitoring.

## Goals
- Consolidate key Windows protection status.
- Surface relevant device/process/startup/network changes.
- Monitor user-selected files for integrity changes.
- Explain findings with evidence and limitations.
- Provide local reports and safe next steps.

## Non-goals
- Antivirus or EDR replacement.
- Guaranteeing a device is malware-free.
- Offensive testing or exploitation.
- Covert surveillance.
- Credential, keystroke or private-content collection.
- Automatic quarantine/deletion or security-control changes.

## Success measures
- Every status has a source and timestamp.
- Unknown is never represented as healthy.
- Recommendations explain why they exist.
- Network discovery stays within confirmed authorized scope.
- File monitoring watches only user-selected paths.
- App has low overhead and reliable recovery.

## Language rules
Say “No issue detected by this check”, not “You are completely secure”.
Say “Unknown publisher”, not “Malicious app”.
Say “File changed”, not “File infected”.
Say “Unknown network device”, not “Attacker”.
